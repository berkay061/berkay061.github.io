Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in the FOLD Android game. The developer responsible for the game is Berkay Alkan (Red Blood). For privacy questions or requests, contact redbloodsupport@gmail.com.

You can play without creating an account. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. FOLD uses Google AdMob for advertisements and Google Play Billing through Unity In-App Purchasing for an optional, one-time remove-ads purchase.

1. Information stored on your device

FOLD saves completed campaign and challenge levels, stars, best move counts, daily-puzzle completion dates, unlocked worlds, virtual petals, hint balances, owned and selected paper, dot and background styles, and language, sound, music volume, vibration and dark-mode preferences locally. These records allow the game to resume progress, apply your choices and manage rewards. The session counter used to control advertising frequency is held in memory and resets when the application restarts.

There is no player registration or developer-operated cloud-save service. We do not receive a copy of your saved puzzles or progress on our own servers. Your operating system and backup settings may separately allow application data to be backed up or restored.

2. Advertising and consent

FOLD uses Google AdMob for interstitial advertisements at breaks between puzzle attempts and optional rewarded advertisements that grant hints when available. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (including to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This processing supports advertising, analytics, measurement and fraud prevention and depends on consent, device settings and SDK configuration. Test advertisements may also involve network requests and SDK data processing.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent where applicable. When required and available, use Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices (also available under Shop → Extras) to revisit your choices. You can also use the advertising controls in Android settings. Refusing personalized advertisements does not necessarily stop all advertisements or all associated data processing. Withdrawal of consent affects subsequent processing and does not reverse processing already lawfully performed.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and AdMob's data disclosures.

3. Permissions, virtual items and purchases

The current game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location. Approximate location inferred by advertising services from an IP address is different from GPS access. Network access is used by advertising and consent services; vibration supports optional game feedback.

Virtual petals, hint bundles and cosmetics use earned in-game currency and are recorded locally. The optional Remove ads product is a one-time Google Play purchase that removes between-puzzle advertisements; optional rewarded hint advertisements remain available. Google handles checkout and payment details. FOLD does not receive your full payment-card number.

Google Play Billing and Unity In-App Purchasing process product and price information, purchase status, transaction identifiers, purchase tokens and receipts to complete, acknowledge and restore purchases and prevent fraud. FOLD checks the purchase receipt on your device and caches the owned product identifier locally. The app checks active purchases again when the store is available; refunded or revoked purchases may remove the entitlement. Unity's purchasing SDK can also process installation/device identifiers, IP addresses, device and app information, purchase interactions and diagnostic information for service operation, measurement and fraud prevention. FOLD does not operate a purchase-validation server, use Unity sign-in or offer external payment providers. Unity advertising and analytics consent flags are set to denied.

Read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity In-App Purchasing privacy information. Restoring purchases requires the Google Play account that owns the product. Clearing local game data does not cancel that purchase.

4. Support and processing purposes

The feedback screen prepares the game version, package identifier, language, dark-mode preference and, during a puzzle, puzzle identifier and move count. These details are copied or included in an email draft only when you choose the corresponding action; FOLD does not automatically send a feedback report.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Support correspondence is handled through Google's email service.

Where a legal basis is required, the applicable basis depends on the activity and your location: providing a service you request, consent for processing that requires consent, legitimate interests in support and preventing abuse, or compliance with legal obligations. Advertising providers explain their own processing in their privacy information.

5. Sharing, retention and security

Google processes advertising, consent and purchase information and handles support email as described above. Unity processes purchasing SDK information as described in section 3. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. We do not operate a separate service to sell your saved game progress or personal information. Advertising-related collection and sharing by Google remain as described in section 2.

Local records remain until deleted or overwritten; operating-system backups may remain separately. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, handle related disputes and meet legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Google and Unity retain advertising, consent, purchase and service records under their respective policies. Clearing or uninstalling the game does not automatically delete these providers' records.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Service providers may process information outside your country; their policies describe applicable safeguards and international processing.

6. Your choices and rights

To remove local game records, use Android's settings for FOLD and clear its application storage/data. This can permanently erase your progress and preferences. Backups may need to be managed separately. There is no FOLD account to delete.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Send requests about information we handle to redbloodsupport@gmail.com. We may ask for information necessary to verify the request. We cannot remotely access progress stored only on your device or directly erase Google's records, but can help identify the relevant provider's request route. Google offers additional controls through its Data & privacy settings.

7. Young players

FOLD is intended for players aged 13 and older. The game does not ask for or store a name, date of birth or age-group selection. Starting with version 1.0.9, the game uses Google's standard UMP consent flow without applying a blanket child-directed or under-age-of-consent classification to every player. The G maximum ad-content rating remains in place. Where required, Google UMP presents regional consent choices; this is not an age-verification service. Version 1.0.7 applies restricted advertising settings to all players. Google Play's account, parental-approval and payment controls apply to purchases. If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate.

8. This website and policy updates

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or tracking cookies to this page. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link is subject to that site's practices.

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes and any required new consent will be addressed as required by applicable law.

FOLD; tamamlanan ana oyun ve meydan okuma bölümlerini, yıldızları, en iyi hamle sayılarını, günlük bulmaca tamamlama tarihlerini, açılan dünyaları, sanal yaprak ve ipucu bakiyelerini, sahip olunan ve seçilen kâğıt, nokta ve arka plan görünümlerini ve dil, ses, müzik seviyesi, titreşim ve gece modu tercihlerini yerel olarak kaydeder. Google'ın reklam SDK'sı; reklam, analiz, ölçüm ve dolandırıcılığı önleme amacıyla IP adreslerini (yaklaşık konumu tahmin etmek dahil), uygulama ve reklam etkileşimlerini, tanılama/performans bilgilerini ve Android reklam kimliği ile uygulama grubu kimliği dahil cihaz veya hesap tanımlayıcılarını toplayabilir ve paylaşabilir. İşleme; izinlere, cihaz ayarlarına ve SDK yapılandırmasına bağlıdır. Test reklamları da ağ istekleri ve SDK üzerinden veri işleme içerebilir.

Gerekli ve kullanılabilir olduğunda Mağaza → Ekstralar bölümündeki gizlilik tercihleri üzerinden seçimlerinizi değiştirebilirsiniz. Android ayarlarındaki reklam kontrollerini de kullanabilirsiniz. Rızayı geri çekmek sonraki işlemleri etkiler; daha önce hukuka uygun yapılmış işlemleri geriye dönük olarak ortadan kaldırmaz.

Ayrıntılar: Google Gizlilik Politikası, Google'ın iş ortağı uygulamalarındaki bilgileri kullanımı ve AdMob veri açıklamaları.

3. İzinler, sanal ürünler ve satın almalar

Mevcut oyun; kişilerinize, kameranıza, mikrofonunuza veya hassas GPS konumunuza erişim istemez. Reklam hizmetlerinin IP adresinden yaklaşık konum çıkarması GPS erişiminden farklıdır. Ağ erişimi reklam ve izin hizmetleri için kullanılır; titreşim isteğe bağlı oyun geri bildirimi sağlar.

Sanal yapraklar, ipucu paketleri ve görünümler oyun içinde kazanılan para birimini kullanır ve yerel olarak kaydedilir. İsteğe bağlı Reklamları kaldır ürünü, tek seferlik Google Play satın almasıyla bölüm arası reklamları kaldırır; isteğe bağlı ödüllü ipucu reklamları kalır. Ödeme ekranını ve ödeme bilgilerini Google yönetir. FOLD, ödeme kartınızın tam numarasını almaz.

Google Play Billing ve Unity In-App Purchasing; satın almaları tamamlamak, onaylamak, geri yüklemek ve sahteciliği önlemek için ürün/fiyat bilgilerini, satın alma durumunu, işlem kimliklerini, satın alma belirteçlerini ve makbuzları işler. FOLD makbuzu cihazınızda kontrol eder ve sahip olunan ürün kimliğini yerel olarak saklar. Mağaza kullanılabilir olduğunda etkin satın almalar yeniden kontrol edilir; iade edilen veya iptal edilen satın almaların hakkı kaldırılabilir. Unity satın alma SDK'sı, hizmetin işletilmesi, ölçüm ve sahteciliği önleme için kurulum/cihaz kimliklerini, IP adreslerini, cihaz ve uygulama bilgilerini, satın alma etkileşimlerini ve tanılama bilgilerini de işleyebilir. FOLD bir satın alma doğrulama sunucusu işletmez, Unity ile oturum açma veya harici ödeme sağlayıcısı kullanmaz. Unity reklam ve analiz izin bayrakları reddedilmiş olarak ayarlanır.

Ayrıntılar: Unity Oyun Oyuncusu ve Uygulama Kullanıcısı Gizlilik Politikası ve Unity In-App Purchasing gizlilik bilgileri. Satın almayı geri yüklemek için ürünün sahibi Google Play hesabı gerekir. Yerel oyun verilerini temizlemek satın almayı iptal etmez.

Geri bildirim ekranı oyun sürümünü, paket kimliğini, dili, gece modu tercihini ve bulmaca sırasında bölüm kimliği ile hamle sayısını hazırlar. Bilgiler yalnızca ilgili düğmeye bastığınızda kopyalanır veya e-posta taslağına eklenir; FOLD otomatik olarak geri bildirim raporu göndermez.

Şifre veya ödeme kartı bilgisi göndermeyin. Destek yazışmaları Google'ın e-posta hizmeti üzerinden işlenir.

Hukuki dayanak gereken durumlarda geçerli dayanak faaliyete ve bulunduğunuz yere bağlıdır: talep ettiğiniz hizmeti sunmak, rıza gerektiren işlemler için rıza, destek ve kötüye kullanımı önlemeye ilişkin meşru menfaatler veya yasal yükümlülükler.
